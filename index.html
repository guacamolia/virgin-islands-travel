Good to know

A few logistics
worth knowing.

St. John

There is no airport on St. John, so the island is reached by ferry or private boat from St. Thomas.

  • Start early for Trunk Bay.
  • Parking at popular beaches can fill quickly.
  • Bring water, sunscreen and snorkeling gear.
  • Leave time to enjoy Cruz Bay before returning.

St. Croix

St. Croix is intentionally separated from the St. Thomas portion of the trip so that it feels like its own island experience rather than a rushed add-on.

  • Travel to St. Croix on Wed 25.
  • Use the island days for exploring and beach time.
  • Keep the final day light before the direct flight.
  • Fly home from St. Croix on Fri 27.

Flying after diving

The itinerary intentionally leaves a full day between the final dive and the flight home. DAN's current guidance suggests a minimum surface interval of 18 hours after multiple dives in a day or multiple days of diving, with longer intervals reducing risk.

  • Boat day: Sun 22
  • St. John days: Mon 23 / Tue 24
  • Flight home: Fri 27

One important distinction

Trunk Bay is the snorkeling stop on the St. John day. The diving portion of the trip belongs on St. Croix, rather than treating Trunk Bay as a scuba site.

  • St. Thomas → boat day + arrival
  • St. John → hiking + snorkeling
  • St. Croix → island exploring
  • Fri 27 → direct flight home
